GraphRAG index structure

graphrag-zero chunks each document, embeds it, then runs hierarchical community detection (Leiden-style) over the chunk graph. Each level is a coarser clustering of the level below.

Level-1 community sizes

Each cell is one of the 252 top-level communities; area is proportional to the number of indexed chunks it contains. Larger cells are broad, well-covered themes; smaller cells are niche topics.

What’s inside the largest communities

graphrag-zero numbers communities rather than naming them. These labels are computed objectively — for each of the 15 biggest level-1 communities, the two source documents contributing the most chunks. The mass of the corpus sits in the comprehensive multi-topic society guidelines.
