# Research-Level Test Questions

Use this file for broader synthesis or cross-document reasoning tests.

## Research Question 1
- Question: Generate 5 research paper topics for plausible mechanisms of rotors in atrial fibrillation pathology.
- Scope (population/intervention/comparator/outcomes, if applicable):
  - Population: Adults with atrial fibrillation (paroxysmal, persistent, and long-standing persistent phenotypes)
  - Focus: Mechanistic electrophysiology and substrate biology underpinning rotor initiation, maintenance, and spatial stability
  - Translational angle: Mapping technologies, signal processing approaches, and potential therapeutic implications
- Required synthesis type:
  - Cross-guideline comparison
  - Multi-trial synthesis
  - Consensus/conflict mapping
- Expected sources to surface:
  - Major AF guideline documents (ACC/ESC)
  - Landmark AF mechanism and mapping studies
  - Ablation and outcomes literature where rotor-targeting hypotheses were tested
- Success criteria for answer:
  - Produces exactly 5 distinct and non-overlapping topic titles
  - Each topic includes a one-sentence rationale grounded in plausible pathophysiology
  - Topics span at least 3 mechanistic domains (for example: ion-channel/electrophysiology, fibrosis/structural substrate, mapping methodology, autonomic modulation)
  - Clearly distinguishes hypothesis-generating ideas from established evidence
  - Uses precise scientific language and avoids vague or generic topic phrasing
- Red flags (what would make the answer insufficient):
  - Fewer or more than 5 topics
  - Generic AF topics without specific rotor mechanism focus
  - No rationale linking proposed topic to mechanism plausibility
  - Conflation of guideline recommendations with mechanistic evidence

## Optional Scoring Rubric

- Evidence quality and citation grounding: /5
- Cross-source synthesis depth: /5
